Refund Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. 14-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

Affinova offers a 14-day refund window for initial plan subscriptions. If you find our edge routing or tracking services unsatisfactory within 14 days of your initial payment, you may request a 100% full refund with no questions asked.

2. Subscription Cancellations

You can cancel your subscription at any time via the User Dashboard under Settings > Subscription. Upon cancellation, your account will remain active until the end of the current billing cycle, with no subsequent charges incurred.

3. Requesting a Refund

To request a refund, please email billing@affinova.io with your account email address and transaction reference ID. Approved refunds are credited back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
