Our Foundation & Philosophy

Engineered for Attribution Integrity.

Affinova was founded to address the silent crisis of modern digital marketing: attribution drift. Traditional affiliate links lose up to 25% of conversions due to aggressive browser cookie policies, ad blockers, and slow CDN redirects. We engineered a first-party edge platform that restores accuracy, transparency, and speed.

Absolute Transparency

No inflated numbers or black-box metrics. Affinova records raw server-side clicks and verified conversion postbacks so both affiliates and brands can audit every dollar.

Sub-Millisecond Routing

Traffic is routed through high-performance edge nodes geographically closest to the visitor. Shorter redirect latencies directly translate to higher conversions.

Guaranteed Settlement

Our multi-rail financial pipeline ensures on-time payouts via domestic bank rails, instant UPI, and PayPal without unnecessary processing holds.

Infrastructure Architecture

The Affinova platform runs on a high-performance microservices architecture optimised for India. Every generated link is cached on edge clusters, ensuring zero downtime even during massive viral traffic spikes. Real-time postbacks connect publishers with merchant cart systems through cryptographically signed HMAC payloads.
