Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Affinova collects account registration details (full name, verified email address, phone number, and password hashes). When visitors engage with publisher smart links, our edge nodes log anonymized connection telemetry (IP address, user agent, referring URL, and geographic region) for the strict purpose of fraud mitigation and attribution verification.

2. First-Party Tracking & Cookieless Architecture

Affinova prioritizes user privacy and GDPR/CCPA compliance. We do not sell publisher or visitor behavioral profiles to third-party data brokers. Our server-side click routing generates session-specific hashes rather than intrusive persistent trackers.

3. Data Security

All stored passwords are encrypted using multi-round bcrypt salt algorithms. Transmission of session data is fortified through TLS 1.3. Payout banking coordinates are safeguarded using AES-256 encryption.

4. Publisher Rights

Publishers may review, export, or delete their profile information and tracking histories by contacting privacy@affinova.io or accessing their account settings.
